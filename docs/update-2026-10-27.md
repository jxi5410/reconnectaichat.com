# Site update, 27 October 2026: privacy policy and terms on this domain

Google's OAuth verification wants the privacy policy on the same domain as the home page, and `reconnectaichat.com/privacy` and `/terms` returned 404. The policy and terms were served only by the production API, so the site now carries its own copy of both.

| Address | File | Text |
|---|---|---|
| `https://reconnectaichat.com/privacy` | `site/privacy/index.html` | "Reconnect Privacy Policy", last updated 20 October 2026 |
| `https://reconnectaichat.com/terms` | `site/terms/index.html` | "Reconnect Terms of Service", last updated 22 September 2026 |

## What is copied

- The text between `<main>` and `</main>` of `https://konggu-api-production.up.railway.app/privacy` and `/terms`, fetched on 27 October 2026, word for word, with the same headings and effective dates. Only markup differs: the privacy page's closing `<footer>` line is a paragraph here, and the terms are broken into one block per line.
- Everything else is the site's own: the ring header, the black-and-white palette (Night through `prefers-color-scheme`), the system font stack and the company footer. No JavaScript, no web fonts, no external requests.
- The footer on every page (home, 404, privacy, terms) now links `/privacy` and `/terms`.
- `site/sitemap.xml` lists both pages.

## Two copies: keep them equal

The source of truth is the server template (`server/app/templates/privacy/` in the app repository), which the App Store listing still points to. Those Railway addresses are not redirected and keep resolving. When the policy or the terms change there, change `site/privacy/index.html` or `site/terms/index.html` in the same release, with the same text and the same "last updated" date. The gate cannot compare the two.

## Gate changes (`tools/check.py`)

- Both pages are required files. They need the canonical address, the exact title, `lang="en"`, one `<h1>`, one inline stylesheet and the doctype. Every page must link `/privacy` and `/terms`.
- Allowed hosts: `www.apple.com` is added (the Apple licence link in the terms); the Railway host is removed, so a page that links back to it fails.
- Allowed `mailto:` links: `hello@reconnectaichat.com` and `xj@reconnectaichat.com` (the policy's and the terms' contact).
- The draft-marker check matches whole words, so "todos" in the policy's list of content is not read as TODO.

## Not on these pages yet

- The 25 October privacy changes (PP1: no audio-retention choices, WhatsApp wording removed) are not in the live text, so they are not here.
- The follow-ups wording (Limited Use disclosure, calendar write access) goes in with the feature, in the server template first.
