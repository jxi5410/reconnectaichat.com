| Footer | "Copyright © 2026 Instinct" left; "Privacy policy", "Terms of service" right. Contact email appears on a sub-page. |

Whole page is 1.9 KB of HTML. The point of the design: the product is described like a person would describe it, and the only thing to do is start.

## 2. The proposed page

Same five elements, Reconnect's brand instead of Instinct's.

**Mark.** The Reconnect ring (closed ring, #B9D0FB→#5F84E6, 18 % band, lightest upper-left), 40 px, top-left, drawn with CSS so it is sharp at any size. No wordmark on the page itself; the browser tab, Open Graph image and footer carry the name.

**Prose (draft, English only).**

> **Reconnect is an assistant that remembers your conversations. It records the meetings you have in person, keeps every word as it was said, and writes down what was decided and who promised what.**
>
> Ask it anything afterwards. What did we agree on pricing? When does the pilot start? What did Maya think about the timeline? It answers from what was actually said, and every answer shows the exact line so you can check it. Your own notes stay separate from the record, so it never puts words in anyone's mouth.
>
> Nothing you capture expires. Every meeting stays searchable for as long as you want it, on the free plan too. It is built for people whose important conversations happen away from a desk.

Why these three: paragraph 1 says what it is (capture, original kept, summary with decisions and actions). Paragraph 2 is the differentiator (grounded answers with the exact line; Context never mixed with meeting evidence). Paragraph 3 is the positioning decision from 3 Oct (history is never locked; in-person professionals).

**Call to action.** One text link with a gradient underline in the ring's colours (our equivalent of the brush stroke). The wording depends on decision 1 below. The recommended launch wording is "Get Reconnect for iPhone".

**Footer.** "Copyright © 2026 Reconnect" · Privacy policy · Terms of service · hello@reconnectaichat.com.

## 3. Look

- Background and text are the app's own tokens: page #F7F9FC with the faint #DCE6FC top glow; text #1F2A44, secondary #5B6270; link #2D5BC7. Night mirror via `prefers-color-scheme`: page #0A0D14, glow #172442, link #7FA3F5 (Round M values). No theme toggle on the site.
- Type: the system font only (SF Pro on Apple devices, matching the app; Segoe UI and Roboto elsewhere). No web fonts, so the page makes no third-party requests and ships no font files. 18 px body, 34 em measure. (Revised 7 Oct: the earlier idea of self-hosting Inter is dropped as needless weight.)
- Motion: none by default. The CTA underline thickens on hover. Optional (decision 2): the ring breathes once on load, 1.0→1.05→1.0, respecting reduced-motion.
- Phone: same page, 17 px body, prose starts near the top, footer wraps to two lines.

## 5. Tech and hosting

- Plain HTML and CSS, one file plus assets. No framework, no build step, no JavaScript needed. No analytics, no cookies, so no banner. Lighthouse 100 on every category is the acceptance bar.
- Source lives in its own repository, `jxi5410/reconnectaichat.com` (revised 7 Oct). Reason: the app repo is private, and GitHub Pages serves private repositories only on paid plans; the account's plan is not visible to the tooling here. A public site in a public repo is free on every plan, keeps the app repo private, and gives the site its own release cadence. The repo stays private while Codex builds and is made public at go-live. The app repo is untouched.
- Hosting: GitHub Pages via a small Actions workflow in that repo, custom domain reconnectaichat.com with enforced HTTPS. Free, no new account, deploys on every merge to `main`. Alternatives in decision 3.
- DNS: the owner signs in to the registrar in the built-in browser pane and I add the records (A/AAAA for the apex to GitHub Pages, CNAME for `www`), the same way we did App Store Connect. Propagation can take up to a day.
- Mail: the Google Workspace MX records on the domain stay untouched.
