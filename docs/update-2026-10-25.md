# Site update, 25 October 2026

After a study of who else lets you talk to your past meetings (Granola, Plaud, Otter, Fireflies, TwinMind, Bee, Limitless, Hedy, jamie, Notion, Copilot, ChatGPT, Gemini, Siri; `docs/redesign/v4/COMPETITION.md` on `redesign/v4` in the app repo), the owner chose, as recommended, to keep the 16 October lead and add the one sentence no rival can say.

| Choice | Picked | Over |
|---|---|---|
| Lead line | **Add "It was there":** "Reconnect is a personal assistant you talk to. It was there for every conversation you recorded, so it knows what you've discussed and what's coming up, and helps you keep on top of what needs to happen next." | Re-leading as "The colleague who was in every conversation. Talk it through, out loud."; leaving the copy as it was |

Only the lead paragraph changed. The meta and share descriptions, the second and third paragraphs, the "Coming next" line, the call to action and the look are as on 16 October.

## Claim checked against the shipped app (main, v2.2)

"It was there for every conversation you recorded": recording and the history ship on every plan, so the sentence holds for every user who has the app today. It claims nothing about conversations the user did not record.
