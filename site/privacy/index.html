Reconnect Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

Reconnect helps you record conversations, keep notes and ask questions about your day. This policy explains the information used to provide those features.

The short version

  • Your recordings, messages and notes are used to provide Reconnect. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising or tracking across other apps and websites.
  • Cloud storage and AI processing can take place outside the United Kingdom. We do not claim that your data stays in the UK.
  • You choose when to record and which optional calendar, photo and messaging features to connect.
  • You can export your content, delete conversations and request deletion of your account in the app.

What we collect

Account and preferences
Your sign-in identifier and email address, and the profile information you choose to save, such as a name, username or phone number. We also keep account preferences, language choices, assistant settings and authorization records needed to run your account.
Recordings and voice conversations
Audio you record or import, including voice notes sent through a linked messaging channel. Recording in the app starts when you choose it and displays a recording indicator. During live Talk, your voice is streamed to an AI provider rather than saved as a meeting recording. Written turns can be kept in chat history.
Content
Your transcripts, original transcription evidence, summaries and their versions, questions and answers, notes, saved Context, templates, todos, speaker labels, photos and attachments. Content can contain information about other people; make sure you have permission to record and share it.
Calendar information
If you allow calendar access, event titles, times, locations, notes and attendee names or contact identifiers used for meeting context and upcoming-meeting features. Calendar access is optional and read-only; Reconnect does not edit or delete your calendar events.
Purchases and usage
App Store transaction and subscription information, account-linked recording and voice usage, and processing costs needed to apply your plan, prevent abuse and operate the service. Apple handles payment; Reconnect does not receive your payment card details.
Connected messaging
If you link WhatsApp, your WhatsApp number and profile display name, linking and delivery identifiers, message text and voice notes, replies and connection preferences.
Diagnostics and reports
App version and build, device model and operating system, crash stacks and error categories, performance and queue metrics, and request metadata. If you send feedback, we receive your description, optional screenshot and selected recent diagnostic logs. A screenshot or description can include content you choose to send; check it before submitting.

Guest conversations

You can chat before signing in, by typing or by talking. We create an isolated guest credential and use Apple App Attest for device verification, including a device-bound key identifier and verification counters. Your questions and limited conversation context go to the providers needed to answer. Guest history is held on this device and may be lost after uninstalling. Guest cloud content is scheduled for deletion after 30 days of inactivity or when you choose Delete guest conversations. Device-verification keys, usage counters and hashed session/network identifiers enforce trial limits and prevent abuse; deleting content does not reset the allowance. When you sign in, imported guest content becomes part of your account and its export and deletion controls apply. Recording or file import may require sign-in or an available guest allowance.

AI, speech and search providers

Reconnect sends content to the providers needed for the feature and model you use. These requests include prompts, language and model settings, and technical request metadata. The available providers depend on service configuration and your model choice.

  • Transcription: Deepgram and AssemblyAI receive recording audio or live audio, language hints and vocabulary terms to make transcripts. Google Gemini also supports audio and Context transcription. Older configured transcription paths may use Alibaba Cloud or Volcengine. File transcription can use a temporary link to the audio.
  • Summaries and answers: OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic or xAI receive your question and conversation context, transcript passages, titles, dates, speaker labels, saved Context, notes or calendar details needed for the response. A configured fallback provider can handle a request when the primary provider fails.
  • Live Talk: your voice is streamed to an AI provider, Google Gemini, which also receives assistant instructions, your preferred name and relevant meeting and saved Context passages. Google can generate both text and voice responses.
  • Spoken replies: When your assistant speaks to you, the words of its reply are sent to a specialist speech provider, which turns them into audio. ElevenLabs receives reply text and voice, language and model settings; it does not receive your microphone audio through this speech-generation path. Google Gemini provides the fallback speech path and voice samples, and separately receives user audio through live Talk and transcription. Reply text can include details from your conversations, notes and calendar. Reconnect does not keep the audio of spoken replies as a durable recording; playback uses temporary files and memory.
  • Search: a private embedding service can receive search queries and transcript or Context passages. Its hosting provider processes those requests. Downloading Google's EmbeddingGemma model weights does not send your text to Google for embedding. If an operator enables the alternative OpenAI embedding path, those queries and passages go to OpenAI. Configured Cohere reranking receives the query and candidate passages already filtered to your permitted content.

Talk speaks by default. Tapping the speaking ring mutes playback for that conversation and tapping again restores sound for subsequent answers. Mute is local to the conversation; it does not stop AI processing or guarantee that reply text is withheld from a speech provider. Leaving Talk ends the mute. Choosing “Not right now” during the welcome quiets that welcome only.

If you select on-device transcription where available, Apple's speech recognition handles that transcription on the device. This does not prevent the resulting transcript or retained recording from being uploaded for other selected features. If you connect your own model provider, the app sends the relevant prompts and content directly to that provider using your key; that provider's terms apply.

Providers can retain requests and responses under their own applicable terms and account settings. We do not promise zero retention, a particular processing country or a universal no-training arrangement. Contact us for the current configured provider list and applicable arrangements.

WhatsApp

Linking WhatsApp is optional. Messages travel through WhatsApp's (Meta's) servers. Your assistant sends short answers and links, never recordings, transcripts or full summaries. Those short answers and link labels can still contain information from your content. Incoming voice notes go through Reconnect's recording and transcription pipeline and count against your hours. Pausing replies stops answers there; Unlink removes the connection. Unlinking does not erase messages already held by you, Meta or other recipients, and does not delete imported recordings from Reconnect.

Storage, diagnostics and access

Apple handles App Store purchases, Apple sign-in and device attestation. If you choose Google or Microsoft sign-in, that identity provider handles authentication and returns account identity information. Email sign-in sends your address and a one-time code through the configured mail delivery provider. Choosing Save to Contacts adds the assistant contact you request; Reconnect does not upload your address book.

Reconnect's API, database, background processing, private search service, object storage and backups are operated by cloud infrastructure providers, including Railway where configured. Stored information includes account data, content, recordings, attachments, exports, feedback and operational records. S3-compatible storage can be supplied by different operators; the protocol alone does not identify a provider or region. Storage and processing can take place outside the United Kingdom; locations depend on the configured services.

Sentry receives scrubbed crash and error reports tagged with app or server release and environment, with code stack locations where available. Error reports exclude transcript text, audio, credentials, request bodies, screenshots and account identity. Automatic screen capture, view hierarchy and content-bearing breadcrumbs are disabled. Diagnostic logs use limited operational fields rather than transcript text or credentials. Service providers necessarily receive connection metadata, such as an IP address, when receiving a request.

Authorized administrators can investigate accounts, feedback and operational errors. Access to transcript text, summaries or audio requires a recorded reason and is audited. We use usage records, rate limits, feature switches and spend limits to operate the service and prevent abuse.

How long we keep information

  • Your saved transcripts, summaries, versions, notes and other account content remain until you delete them or your account.
  • Where enforced by the service, Keep audio offers Always, 1 year, 90 days, 30 days or Don't keep for new recordings. The period starts when the summary is published; Don't keep schedules deletion no earlier than 24 hours later. Changing the setting does not change earlier recordings. When retention enforcement is unavailable, the app shows Always. After audio is removed, the transcript, summary and notes remain.
  • Deleting a conversation first moves it to Recently deleted where supported. Permanent deletion removes its content and queues stored files for deletion.
  • Live Talk audio and generated reply audio are not retained by Reconnect as meeting recordings. Written chat turns can remain in history. Transcription evidence and provisional processing records can remain with the meeting until its deletion.
  • Feedback, account-linked operational records, and provider-held information have their own retention and deletion arrangements. We do not state a fixed backup or provider erasure period without verified configuration and terms. Copies in backups may remain until backup expiry; deleting active data does not instantly erase backups or copies held by other recipients.

Your choices, export and deletion

You can change optional permissions in iOS Settings, disconnect calendars and messaging, export your account data, and delete content or your account from Reconnect Settings. Account export includes the account's owned content and associated available files. Files already removed under your audio-retention choice cannot be recovered by export.

Account deletion revokes access and removes account-owned database content. Remaining object files are placed in a durable deletion queue with a stated 24-hour deletion deadline. Failed deletions are retried and completion is recorded after storage confirms deletion. This deadline concerns active Reconnect storage, not copies retained independently by providers or backups. Device-bound, pseudonymous abuse-prevention records can remain so deletion does not reset a guest allowance.

You can contact us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or a portable copy of your personal information, as applicable to you. You can also raise a concern with your local data protection authority. Export before deleting your account if you want a copy.

Children

Reconnect is not directed at children under 16. If you believe a child has supplied personal information, contact us.
